Bored?

Alpha: Bored is new and still changing. Share what you think

Bored is an app to make life more fun.

Instead of scrolling through ads in a “social” media app, you can swipe through opportunities to actually socialize with other people! Use it for anything: go for a walk with a bored neighbor; find another bored parent and meet up at the park with your bored kids; spend some time with a friend who's doing a boring, monotonous task, like packing or painting; or meet at a cafe so you can have a nice cup of coffee and scroll through social media together!

How it works

  1. Connect

    Open your profile, create an invitation, and send it to your friends to invite them to Bored.

  2. Post

    Post something you want to do or need help with. Optionally include details like where, when, and how much, or leave them blank and play it by ear. Share it with everyone, or just with people you know.

  3. Discover

    Swipe through events—right means “I'm in.” Filter by tags, distance, date, and more. Once you've swiped, the host decides whether they want to go ahead. Don't be offended if nothing happens; this is a guilt-free zone. Maybe the host changed their mind, or maybe the timing isn't right anymore.

  4. Confirm

    As a host, you swipe through the people who want to join—right means “Let's do it.” There's no obligation: if you've lost interest or just aren't feeling it, that's OK. If you don't like turning people down, be more specific about what you want in your posts and share them with a more targeted audience.

  5. Plan

    It's a match! Chat to sort out the remaining details, tap or click “When” to add it to your calendar, and then go do the thing!

Can't I just text my friends if I want to hang out with them?

You can. But if you texted all your friends about every little whim or need, you'd probably overwhelm them and get left on read.

With Bored, you post it once. Whoever's up for it says so, and nobody else gets pestered. You can still keep it to people you know: share a post with just your contacts.

What's wrong with other apps?

They're businesses. They exist to make money, and you are the product that they sell to their advertisers. They need your name, email, phone number, and contact list to build a creepily accurate profile of you, so they can send you targeted ads that you're more likely to click on. But that's not all! They want more money, so they also nag you to subscribe, upgrade, and get verified, and they charge extra for paywalled “premium” features. Profit is the priority, and they provide the minimum viable product to keep you on their platform. Eventually, enshittification sets in, the platform slowly dies, and another service pops up and goes through the whole cycle again.

Bored doesn't do any of that, because Bored isn't a business. It was made with passion, by people for people, not by a corporation for profit.

What's special about Bored?

  • ✓

    No Ads

    Nobody is paying to put things in front of you, and nobody ever will. Bored isn't made for money. It's made because software can and should be better!

  • ✓

    No Investors

    No one to answer to who wants a return on your attention. No venture capitalists. No money. No perverse incentives. Just software built to solve a human problem and given away for free, because being generous feels good.

  • ✓

    No EULA

    You don't have to get lawyers involved to use a screwdriver, so why should you have to sign an End User License Agreement (EULA) to use a piece of software? This is a tool, not a legally binding agreement. Bored won't ask you to click “I agree.”

  • ✓

    Privacy Enforced

    No email addresses, no phone numbers, no full names (please), and no photos with their tricksy metadata. Bored makes it as hard as possible for you to dox yourself by not collecting any data that isn't necessary. Additionally, your private posts, requests to join, matches, and chats are client-side end-to-end (E2E) encrypted as thoroughly as Bored can manage, so the server can't see who's doing what with whom. Only public posts are readable by the server, and they're signed, so nobody can fake one. See the privacy policy for everything Bored stores, and the encryption spec for how it works.

  • ✓

    Free Libre Open Source Software (FLOSS)

    Free forever: no premium tier and no paywalled features. Licensed under the GNU AGPL, so you can take the code, change it, and run your own. Just keep it free for the next person, too.

How can Bored exist without making any money?

Bored costs almost nothing to run, since it doesn't store photos or use much network bandwidth. It was created for fun to solve a need, and it runs on a tiny, bare-metal cluster. No cloud bill. It only costs a few dollars a month for electricity.
