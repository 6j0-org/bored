Bored?

Bored is an app to make life more fun.

Instead of scrolling through ads in a "social" media app, you can swipe through opportunities to actually socialize with other people! Use it for anything: go for walk with a bored neighbor; find another bored parent, and meet up at the park with your bored kids; spend some time with a friend who's doing some boring, monotonous task, like packing or painting; or meet at a cafe, so you can have a nice cup of coffee, and scroll through social media together!

Socializing doesn't have to mean excess calories, booze, or cash. A walk, a board game, a pickup game in the park. Bored helps you find people to do whatever it is you want to do.

How it works

  1. Post

    What, where, when, how much (or free), and how many people. Share it with everyone, or just with people you know.

  2. Discover

    Swipe through what's happening near you. Right means “I'm in.” Filter by tags, distance, date, or free.

  3. Confirm

    Hosts swipe through who wants to come. Right means “Let's do it.”

  4. Plan

    It's a match. Chat to sort out the details, end-to-end encrypted. Then go do the thing.

What's wrong with other event sites?

They're businesses. They make money from ads, subscriptions, ticket fees, or investors who expect a return, and most of them want to know who you are: your name, your face, your email, your phone number, your address.

Bored doesn't, because Bored isn't a business.

  • MeetupOrganizers pay a subscription to run a group, members are offered a paid Meetup+ tier, and organizers can make you wait for approval or require a profile photo to join.
  • Facebook Groups & EventsReal-name profiles, paid for by ads built on what you do.
  • EventbriteBuilt for selling tickets, not for meeting people, with fees added to paid tickets.
  • EviteFree invitations come with ads. Ad-free designs cost extra.
  • Paperless PostPremium designs cost “Coins” you buy, and you need an account to send.
  • PartifulBuilt around your phone number. Backed by venture capital.
  • LumaTakes 5% of paid tickets unless the host pays for a Luma Plus subscription.
  • Bumble For FriendsProfile photos up front, with paid upgrades for more.
  • NextdoorWants to verify your home address. Ad-supported.
  • PeanutPhoto profiles and selfie verification for moms, with a paid Peanut Plus tier.
  • TimeleftDinner with strangers, for a membership or ticket fee on top of your meal.

Can't I just text my friends if I want to hang out with them?

You can, but if you texted all your friends every time you wanted to do any little thing, you'd probably overwhelm them and get left on read.

With Bored, you post it once. Whoever's up for it says so, and nobody else gets pestered. You can still keep it to people you know: share a post with just your contacts.

Bored's promises

  • ✓

    Free forever

    No premium tier. No paywalled features. Starting things and joining things cost nothing.

  • ✓

    Free libre open source

    Licensed under the GNU AGPL. Take the code, change it, run your own. Just keep it free for the next person, too.

  • ✓

    Ad-free, always

    Nobody is paying to put things in front of you, and nobody ever will.

  • ✓

    No investors, ever

    No one to answer to who wants a return on your attention.

  • ✓

    Private by design

    You can't leave personal info, because Bored doesn't collect it. Sign in with a passkey. Names you give contacts are encrypted in your browser, and chats are end-to-end encrypted.

Things Bored never asks for, so you can't accidentally dox yourself:

  • Photos
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Real name
  • Password

How can Bored keep these promises?

Bored is very lightweight. It doesn't store photos or use much network bandwidth, so it costs almost nothing to run.

It was made with passion, by people for people, not by a corporation for profit.
